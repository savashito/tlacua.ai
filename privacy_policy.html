Tlacua.ai Privacy Policy

Effective Date: December 16, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Tlacua.ai ("we," "us," or "our") handles personal data in connection with your use of the Tlacua.ai WhatsApp Bot (the "Bot"). Our fundamental commitment is Zero Data Retention regarding your conversation content.

1. Core Principle: Zero Data Retention (Chat Content)

Our service is built on the principle of privacy and minimizing data collection.

A. Conversation Content:

  • We do not store, retain, log, or save any text, documents, or prompts you submit to the Bot.
  • The content of your conversations is processed locally within your environment or securely discarded immediately after the AI model generates a response.
  • We cannot access, review, or use your conversation history for training, research, marketing, or any other purpose.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

In addition to conversation content, we do not intentionally collect the following personal data through the Bot service:

  • Names, email addresses, or specific user identifiers (other than the necessary WhatsApp ID required for the session).
  • IP addresses or device information related to your use of the Bot service (this data is handled by WhatsApp).
  • Sensitive personal data (as defined by GDPR or LFPDPPP).

3. Limited Information We Process

Because the Bot operates on WhatsApp, we necessarily process minimal information essential for service function and basic business operations:

Category of Data Purpose of Processing Legal Basis (GDPR/EU) Retention Period
A. WhatsApp Session Data
(Your phone number, profile ID, message timestamps)		 To initiate and maintain the chat session, deliver the Bot's response, and manage message throughput. Legitimate Interest / Contractual Necessity Discarded after message delivery or session ends (subject to WhatsApp's own policy).
B. Aggregate Usage Metrics
(Total messages processed, error rates, model performance statistics)		 Service improvement, performance monitoring, and resource optimization (hardware and SLMs). Legitimate Interest Anonymized and aggregated data is retained indefinitely; individual event data is retained for 30 days for troubleshooting.
C. Opt-in Learning Data
(Only if the feature is explicitly enabled by the user/organization)		 To fine-tune and improve the domain-specific SLMs for that user/organization. Consent Until consent is withdrawn or the learning objective is achieved.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information is only shared under the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We may share anonymous, aggregated usage metrics with service providers necessary to operate the service (e.g., cloud infrastructure providers), but never with access to individual conversation content.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, or legal process in the jurisdiction of Tlacua.ai (Mexico/USA/EU).

5. Your Rights and Compliance

Tlacua.ai respects the privacy rights of all users, particularly those governed by GDPR and Mexico's LFPDPPP. Given our Zero Data Retention policy for conversation content, specific rights related to that content are moot.

However, regarding the minimal data we do process (Section 3):

  • Right of Access/Rectification (ARCO Rights): You have the right to request access to the limited, aggregated data we hold about your usage (Section 3B). Since we do not retain identifiable conversation data, we cannot retrieve or modify it.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): You may request the deletion of the limited identifiable session data we may temporarily hold.
  • Right to Opt-out (Consent): Any optional features, such as opt-in learning, require explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

6. Security

We implement robust technical and organizational measures, including the use of guardrails and secure local processing techniques, to protect the data we handle (Section 3) from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

7. International Data Transfers

As a service designed for local processing and zero data retention, the risk of international transfer of personal communication data is minimized. Any necessary processing of aggregate usage metrics (Section 3B) will be done in compliance with GDPR and LFPDPPP requirements, ensuring adequate safeguards are in place.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this document.
